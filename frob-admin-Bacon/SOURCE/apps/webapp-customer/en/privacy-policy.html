Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

What We Collect

When you book a tour, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and country of residence. Payment card details are processed directly by Stripe and never touch our servers.

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to process bookings, send confirmation and reminder emails, and respond to enquiries. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Retention

Personal data is automatically anonymised 90 days after your booking date. Financial records (amounts, booking references) are retained for 6 years per HMRC requirements.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at hello@friendsonbikes.uk.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for site functionality and optional analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) with your consent. You can manage your preferences via the cookie banner at any time.
