Our Story

About Friends on Bikes

Two Locals, One Mission

The best way to see a city is by bike, with someone who lives there.

We're Alex and Sam, Barbican residents who have spent years exploring London's streets, parks, and hidden corners on two wheels. The Barbican isn't just our base — it's where we live. We cycle these roads every day, and we know which alley cuts through to which courtyard, which pub has stood on its corner since the seventeenth century, and which Roman ruin hides under the car park on Aldersgate Street.

We started Friends on Bikes after meeting visitors who wanted something more personal than a coach tour and more informative than a self-guided map — people who'd done the museums and wanted to actually understand the city. We designed three routes for them.

Every route has been ridden by us hundreds of times. We refine them on guest feedback, reroute around roadworks, and test every story to make sure it lands. We keep groups to a maximum of 10, because a great tour is a conversation, not a broadcast.

What We Believe

Our Values

1

Small groups, always

We will never take more than 10 riders on an open tour. It's a deliberate decision we won't reverse. Guides can tell stories to 10 people; they broadcast to 20.

2

Genuinely local content

Our guides are qualified cycling leaders with a genuine obsession with London history. Our stories come from years of research and living in the City — not a script written in an office elsewhere.

3

Safety as standard

Routes prioritise cycle paths, quiet back streets, and riverside roads. Every guide carries a first aid kit and is trained in cycle safety. Bikes are maintained daily and checked before every departure.

4

Premium without the pretension

A fair price for a premium experience. No flash-sale discounting, no last-minute deals, no countdown timers. What you see is what you pay.
