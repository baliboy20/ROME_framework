Our Promise

The Friends on Bikes Difference

We're not a franchise. We're two locals who live and work in the Barbican, and we've designed every route ourselves.

1

Max 10 Guests

Your guide knows your name and can answer every question. We will never take more than 10 riders on an open tour.

2

Genuinely Local Routes

We live in the Barbican. The City is our daily commute — we know every alley, hidden garden and shortcut the coach tours can't reach.

3

Premium Bikes

Quality hybrid bikes, maintained daily before every departure.

4

Everything Included

Bike, helmet, hi-vis vest and printed route map — all included. No hidden fees, ever.

5

Flexible Cancellation

Cancel more than 48 hours before your tour for a full refund. Secure payment by Stripe.

6

All Abilities Welcome

Flat, traffic-calmed routes with quality bikes maintained daily. Ages 12 and up.
