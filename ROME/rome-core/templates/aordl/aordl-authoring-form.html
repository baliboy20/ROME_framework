AORDL Requirement Authoring Form

AI-Optimized Requirement Design Language - Interactive Template

1. Identification

Unique identifier and actor role
Format must be REQ-### (e.g., REQ-001)
Must be a specific role (not "user" or "person")

2. Intent

What does the actor want to do? Format: verb + business-object
Must use approved verb + business object
Approved verbs: create, read, update, delete, submit, approve, reject, cancel, archive, restore, export, import, view, search

3. Preconditions

What must be true BEFORE this intent can execute?

4. Conditions

Contextual conditions that influence execution (optional)

5. Postconditions

What system state changes AFTER successful execution?

6. Outcomes

What observable results can the actor verify?

7. Invariants

Business rules that must ALWAYS be true

8. Non-Functional Requirements

Performance, security, scalability, etc.

9. Errors

Error conditions and user-facing messages

10. Scope Boundary

What's included and excluded?

11. Open Questions

Questions needing clarification or decision

12. Copilot Mode

Validation strictness level
